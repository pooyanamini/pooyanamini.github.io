About

I'm a Staff Machine Learning Engineer at Meta in the San Francisco Bay Area. My recent work focuses on machine learning for Meta Neural Band. Previously, I led a team building training, evaluation, and inference systems for language-model-based sequential decision-making.

I'm especially interested in finding model failures after deployment, measuring them reliably, and using what we learn to improve the next training cycle.

Earlier, I competed in RoboCup's middle-size league, where our team won the engineering challenge at the 2004 world championship in Lisbon, placed fifth at the ACM ICPC Asia regional, and won a silver medal at Iran's National Informatics Olympiad.

Work

  • Machine learning for Meta Neural Band Contributed to the 2025 launch of Meta Neural Band, Meta's first consumer product using surface EMG, bundled with Meta Ray-Ban Display. My work includes personalizing EMG models to improve coverage across the user population, monitoring for population drift in a privacy-preserving way by checking in-product failure rates against offline baselines, and training a single model jointly across six or more interaction tasks. product launch
  • Sequential decision-making with LLMs Led a seven-person team that built a repeatable path from data generation and evaluation through supervised and reinforcement learning to inference for sequential decision-making. This let the team iterate on LLMs for multi-step tasks within one development loop.
  • End-to-end task-oriented dialogue (Project CAIRaoke) Led scalable training and inference for end-to-end task-oriented dialogue, including API-call generation and model-in-the-loop workflows for rapid error correction. project paper
  • Small-business advertiser recommendations Built recommendation models that grew monthly active advertisers while reducing compute costs by about $7M.
  • WhatsApp group integrity Developed spam and automation detection, progressing from statistical anomaly detection to multi-task classifiers deployed at group creation.
  • LTE modem algorithms At Qualcomm, led four engineers on channel estimation and tracking loops, from algorithm design through firmware integration, lab bring-up and field triage. Owned baseband algorithms in two commercial high-end LTE modems, cutting channel-estimation power by 20% and raising the peak bitrate 33% with memory-efficient 256QAM demodulation.
